## Root cause and trace

- `analytics/option_features.py::compute` groups by expiry AND underlying, computes
  BAW IV, and repeats surface summaries across strike rows. ATM IV averages valid
  call/put IVs at the closest strike to the forward. No solver error is established.
- `analytics/history_context.py::_options_metrics` independently selects each day's
  earliest expiry. `analytics/monitor_state.py::build_day_diff` subtracted those
  rolling headline metrics without retaining identity. At rollover that difference
  is not a same-contract daily volatility move. This deterministic defect is confirmed.
- `reporting/daily_report.py::_market_risk_section` independently selected first
  non-null metrics while listing sorted expiries separately. It could mix surfaces
  or fill a missing front IV with a later-expiry value.
- `workflow/packets.py` made these numbers available as canonical cited evidence.
  `.codex/agents/curvelens_synthesizer.toml` and the synthesis task generated by
  `workflow/orchestration.py` required exact citations but no same-contract rule.
- `workflow/finalize.py::_check_key_metrics` checked shapes, digits, and evidence-ID
  membership, not arithmetic or expiry identity. `workflow/completion.py` checks
  identity, presence and SHA256 integrity, which cannot prove interpretation.

## Correction and scope

`analytics/option_comparison.py` selects one deterministic front surface by expiry
and underlying; it never fills holes from a different surface. It matches that
surface in the prior session, rejects nonfinite/conflicting repeated summaries,
and emits null daily deltas when the match is absent. It records both dates,
identity, prior headline identity, rollover flag, decimal and percentage-point
deltas, direction and exact comparison text. `build_day_diff` uses matched ATM IV
and risk-reversal deltas. Headline metrics now come from one selected surface.

Canonical packet rules and both synthesis instruction paths require matched
comparisons. The manifest includes the computed comparison contract. Finalization
requires ATM-IV-labelled snapshot and top-view metrics to copy its exact comparison
text. This protects those displayed comparisons; it is not a general prose theorem
checker. Headlines, summaries, story chains, forecasts, and differently labelled
metrics still need editorial review. Old packets without the contract remain
readable; regenerate them to obtain this guard. No old report is retroactively certified.

Historical percentile context and the expressly named `front_atm_iv` forecast
outcome remain rolling-front series. Changing forecast target semantics requires a
separately versioned forecast contract and is outside this correction.
